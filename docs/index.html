🌐Market Breadth

How much of the market is actually taking part: advancers against decliners, stocks above their 20, 50 and 200-day averages, and names near 52-week highs and lows.

Screens

🎖️TechnoFunda 100

Every listed company scored on outperforming results, relative strength and quality, with GARP, rising-profit and pullback screens built in.
📊PEAD Board

Post-earnings drift: fresh results with strong sales and profit growth, ranked by PEAD score and tracked from the day they were screened.
🪄Magic Formula

Greenblatt's Magic Formula: businesses ranked on return on capital against earnings yield, so quality is bought cheaply.
⚡Intraday

The day's biggest gainers and losers with VWAP and traded volume. Refreshes while the local realtime runner is on.
📦Orders

Order wins and contract awards picked out of company filings, so a large win is read against the size of the business.
💰Buybacks

Tender-offer buybacks with the acceptance-ratio maths done, to estimate what the arbitrage actually returns.
✨Special Sit

Event-driven situations surfaced from corporate filings: the one-offs that move a stock apart from its results.
🔀Demergers

Demergers followed from announcement through NCLT and record date to listing, grouped by stage.

Intrinsic Value

🧭Sector Tailwind

Earnings momentum, breadth, flows and quality rolled up per sector into a headwind or tailwind. Open any sector for its stocks.
🏭Industries

Every listed company rolled up by industry instead of 22 coarse sectors, compared on median P/E, ROCE, margins and growth.
🏅IV Ranking

Good businesses at a sensible price: a growth, quality and value funnel alongside the Magic Formula, with the best in each sector.
🗺️Return Maps

Ten-year price CAGR against each factor, showing which zones of growth, quality and cheapness actually paid off.
⚖️Fair Value

Intrinsic value per stock from a two-stage DCF, ranked by the discount to today's price.

Tools

🎯Valuation

Project earnings forward, value them on an exit P/E, and back-solve the price to pay for the return you want.
🔎Fundamental

Look up any listed company by name or BSE code for the full picture: statements, ratios, signals, documents and the bull/bear debate.
🤖STDRL Scanner

Reinforcement-learning trading agents trained and compared on monthly bars, with each agent's equity curve.

Market Pulse

Where foreign institutional money is going, sector by sector, fortnight by fortnight.
🏦FPI Flows

Foreign portfolio investment by sector over time: which sectors are being bought and which are being sold.
🏛️Banking Data

Listed banks compared side by side on financing margin, return on equity and assets, profit margin and growth.
🚗Auto Sales

Monthly vehicle registrations by maker and vehicle category, with market share and year-on-year change.
🤝Deals

Bulk, block and insider deals: who is buying and selling in size.
📣Announcements

The latest corporate announcements across listed companies, as they are filed.
🎁Corp Actions

Upcoming dividends, bonuses, splits and rights issues across listed companies, with their ex-dates.
